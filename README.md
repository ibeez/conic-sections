# Conic sections

A clear double cone cut by a plane, drawn by hand in code. Drag anywhere on the figure to turn the plane in any direction, and watch the cut change between a circle, an ellipse, a parabola and a hyperbola. Tilt, eccentricity and the curve's name update as you go. The camera slowly circles the cone on its own.

Open it: https://ibeez.github.io/conic-sections/

Works on a phone too. The colours are sampled from real markers.

Made with [Colorfigures](https://april-zhu.com/colorfigures).
